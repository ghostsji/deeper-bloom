# Deeper Bloom design

A first-person survival game in Deep Bloom's glowing sea. You dive, study what lives down there, keep yourself alive, tend a reef from a raft base on the surface, and fight what the blight has turned feral. Desktop only, fullscreen, mouse and keyboard.

## Pillars (the five genres)

| Genre | How it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| **Exploration** | Four zones stacked by depth, carried over from Deep Bloom: Shallows → Kelp Forest → Twilight Trench → Ember Wastes. Each is darker, colder and more dangerous; deeper zones need gear from research. Echo's memory shards hide in each zone and tell the story. |
| **Research** | The Echo Lens scans creatures, coral and plants. Scans fill the codex and earn Data. At a research station on the raft you spend Data to unlock tools, build pieces and dive gear. |
| **Survival** (full) | Breath drains underwater and refills at the surface, on rafts or at air vents. Health takes damage from predators, pressure and cold. Hunger means catching fish and growing kelp, then cooking them on the raft. Gear wears out and is repaired with gathered materials. Blacking out wakes you on your raft and drops what you carried where you fell. |
| **Strategy** (reef management) | No raids. The reef is your economy. Coral gardens, kelp farms and tamed creatures (lantern fish harvest polyps, drift jellies boost growth, as in Deep Bloom) produce Lumen and materials. You decide what to grow, where, and what to put each creature on. A healthier reef pushes the blight back. |
| **FPS** (creature combat) | Predators gone feral from the blight: eels in the kelp, packs of cinder-scaled hunters in the trench, anglers in the dark. You fight with a harpoon, then researched tools (shock spear, flare gun). Fighting is a cost to manage, not the goal: noise and light draw predators. |

## Resources

- **Lumen**: gathered from polyps; spent on building. Coral gardens make more.
- **Data**: earned by scanning; spent on research.
- Later: shell, kelp fibre and fish (food and crafting), and ember glass from the deep zones.

## Controls

WASD swim · mouse look · Space rise or jump · Ctrl or C sink · Q/E turn · Shift dash · 1 harpoon · Tab phone · hold right mouse Echo Lens (hold left mouse to scan) · F use · R eat · Esc or P pause.
Underwater, left-click grabs a silverfin or strips kelp.
Building: pick a piece on the phone, then at the surface left-click to place, right-click to take back, wheel to switch, X to stop.

## The phone

The diver carries Deep Bloom's app on a phone in a dive case, the link between the two games. Tab brings it up and the game pauses. Tabs: Build, Research (works when linked to a research station), Codex and Status (meters, bag, eat).
